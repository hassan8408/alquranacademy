Programs Offered

📖

1. Basic Qaida Course

Perfect for absolute beginners and children. Learn to recognize, read, and connect Arabic letters correctly using standard interactive manuals like Noorani Qaida.

🗣️

2. Nazra & Fluency

Focused on step-by-step reading flow directly within the Holy Quran text, helping you eliminate hesitation and construct beautiful rhythm while reading.

✨

3. Tajweed Rules

Deep-dive instructions into core theoretical and practical pronunciation mechanics, covering Madd rules, Ghunnah accents, and specific letter articulation points.

🕌

4. Islamic Essentials

A comprehensive addition teaching the vital step-by-step method of Namaz (Salah), basic daily Sunnah Duas, 6 Kalimas, and structural Islamic character manners (Akhlaq).
