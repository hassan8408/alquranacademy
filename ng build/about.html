About Al-Quran Academy

Nurturing a deep, lifelong bond with the Holy Quran through authentic scholarship, certified tutors, and personalized one-on-one learning.

OUR STORY

A Journey of Faith, Excellence & Heritage

Founded with the sole objective of making Quranic knowledge accessible to every Muslim home, Al-Quran Academy has grown from a humble tutoring group into a globally recognized online Islamic institution.

We recognize that busy schedules, geographical constraints, and lack of nearby qualified teachers often hinder families in Western and non-Muslim majority countries from accessing quality Islamic education. By bringing together Ijazah-certified male and female scholars with state-of-the-art interactive digital tools, we ensure every student receives dedicated, patient, and loving guidance.

Mission & Vision

The guiding principles that steer our academic and spiritual endeavors each day

Our Mission

To provide highest-quality Quranic and Islamic education to Muslims worldwide, adhering strictly to authentic Tajweed rules (Ahkam-ut-Tajweed) and traditional Prophetic Sunnah, delivered through compassionate, structured, and modern digital classrooms.

Our Vision

To be the most trustworthy, student-centric online Quran academy globally, raising generations of confident reciters, Hafiz-ul-Quran, and knowledgeable individuals who live by the teachings and morals of the Holy Book.
